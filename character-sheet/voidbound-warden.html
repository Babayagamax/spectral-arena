A boss encounters the player once — and is remembered forever.

The VoidboundWarden

A silent sentinel wreathed in spectral violet fire. Its armor predates the kingdom it guards — and the thing coiled around it is not decoration. It is the other half of the boss.

ClassAncient Guardian — Heavy Plate
ElementVoid / Spectral Flame
WeaponGreatsword of the Sunken Oath (planted stance)
DemeanorElegant, patient, absolute
SignatureThe beast is drawn to the blade — both strike together
Plate I — The Warden, at rest
Design Pillars 02
I · Plate

The Armor

Black and silver full plate, layered with sharp overlapping plates and engraved chevron patterns. Violet energy runs through every seam — the armor is not decorated, it is powered.

II · Steel

The Greatsword

An ancient blade planted vertically at rest, taller than a man. Metallic texture with a glowing fuller, engraved runes — and a single gold pommel, the only warm light in the whole design.

III · Spectre

The Beast

Swirling purple spectral energy coils around the Warden like a gigantic ethereal dragon. It is silhouette, threat, and readability all at once — the boss fight's second health bar.

IV · Stage

The Atmosphere

Deep black void, intense violet rim light, volumetric shadow, floating particles. Cinematic key lighting from below and behind — the Warden arrives before the fight begins.
