Privacy Policy — Spectral Arena

Last updated: September 2026

Spectral Arena ("the game") is a single-player arcade game. This policy explains what happens with your data when you play it. The short version: the game collects nothing.

Data We Collect

None. The game does not collect, transmit, store on a server, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no logins, and no analytics.

Data Stored on Your Device

The game saves your high score and settings locally on your device (browser local storage or app storage). This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing your browser data removes it permanently.

Permissions

The game requests no special device permissions. Sound is played only after you interact with the game.

Children's Privacy

The game contains no chat, no user-generated content, and no data collection. It is safe for all ages (stylised fantasy violence, no gore).

Changes to This Policy

If the game ever changes in a way that involves data, this page will be updated here.
